# My favorite series/movies

1. Black Clover – It’s about a boy named Asta who has no magic in a world full of magic, but he works hard to become the Wizard King with his friend and rival, Yuno.
2. Nisekoi – A boy and a girl from rival families pretend to be a couple to keep peace between their gangs, but real feelings slowly start to grow.
3. One Week Friends – A boy befriends a girl who loses her memories of friends every week, and he keeps trying to be close to her again and again.
4. 500 Days of Summer – A man falls in love with a woman who doesn’t believe in true love, and the story shows their happy and sad moments together.
5. Spider-Verse – It’s about a teenager named Miles Morales who gets spider powers and learns to become his own Spider-Man with help from other Spider-heroes from different worlds.
